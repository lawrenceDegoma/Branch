Sync your LeetCode solves to Branch

When a solution you submit on leetcode.com or neetcode.io is accepted, this extension marks the problem solved in Branch, in every group and roadmap that includes it. It also times each problem while you work on it, on the toolbar button, with an optional time budget.

What it reads and sends

Your groups see a solve only if you turn on "Share my solves" in Branch. You can disconnect, or delete synced code, at any time.

Privacy policy

Branch is independent and not affiliated with LeetCode or NeetCode.
